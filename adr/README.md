# Architecture Decision Records

Records follow [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md) and carry their own status and decision date. A
proposed record is not an accepted decision. An accepted record applies to its stated scope;
it does not establish completion or production readiness for the whole system.

Decisions ADR-001…ADR-017 were taken between 13 and 20 August 2026 during the original design
phase. ADR-018…ADR-028 record the earlier implementation. They remain historical evidence;
new-system reading, access and intake decisions begin with ADR-029. Retention does not make
every earlier choice applicable to each new consumer.

A changed decision needs an explicit dated amendment or a superseding ADR, with the previous
decision preserved. Read both the original decision and any amendments: ADR-032 and ADR-034
have scoped extensions, and ADR-038 has separate foundation and reception acceptance. At the
15 September 2026 documentation checkpoint, both ADR-038 scopes are accepted; worker
processing and preparation are not thereby implemented. See the [current contracts](../docs/README.md#current-contracts-and-decisions)
and [board](../BOARD.md) for their consumers and delivery state.
