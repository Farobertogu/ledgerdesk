## Implemented path

1. Receive and accept the original/extraction through the existing producers.
2. Reserve a bounded preparation against exact accepted sources, selection and current treatment.
3. Capture the declared document bytes with admission before each body chunk.
4. Validate selection, retained dependencies, resources, transformation and differences; preserve an immutable preparation revision.
5. Propose one unit or its inseparable group against that exact revision, with explicit target and human judgment.
6. Recheck authority and the inspected item; constitute a candidate, relate an authenticated repetition or persist a typed unresolved block.
7. Reconcile the existing effect under current consultation authority after a lost response. Do not execute again merely because a new client key or proposal wrapper is supplied.

There are separate operations for reserve, upload, finalize, preparation query, resource query, difference query, proposal and constitution, plus the existing operation lookup with an explicit preparation profile. Responses use closed shapes. A public response validator is not a substitute for checking the internal producer.

### Exactness, provenance and limits

`preparation-content/1` conserves exact selected content, source references, positions, resources and associations, components, incidents, coverage, conditions and proposed classification. `prepared-material/2` fixes a descriptor, payload and paired differences. Versioned digest domains avoid self-referential hashes. Integrity does not authenticate origin: actual source acceptance, controlled records and current read/effect admission supply that boundary.

A correction or synthesis is not required to repeat original wording. It must retain its exact antecedents, transformation, affected objects, responsible actor and before/after association. Nonliteral correspondence is explicit. New preparation revisions do not mutate earlier ones, and newer existence alone does not make an older still-admissible confirmation stale.

The limits are finite: 64 KiB command; 8 MiB staged document and prepared payload including selected resource bytes; 8 MiB + 64 KiB response; 64 MiB retained byte pool; 32 attempts; 128 source aliases and units; 10,000 entries/cells; eight sheets; 1,000 CSV records and 64 columns; 5 s idle and 10 s transfer limits. These are profile bounds, not a proof of maximum physical memory, universal extraction completeness or production sizing. No T03 quota is inherited or enlarged.

A real accepted 75,000-line original produced 8,570,205 normalized bytes. Selecting its complete closure exceeded the command/entry bounds and was refused without silently selecting less. That case does **not** claim it reached the prepared-byte constructor. A separate independently sized authored preparation exercised exactly 8,388,608 retained payload bytes successfully and one additional byte with an explicit 413. Full retained capacity was exercised with real staged bytes, then refused with 429 while earlier work remained readable.

### Authority and C9

The adapter reuses the existing session, support, scope, live-control and treatment evaluator. It adds explicit per-operation partitions and M04-D02 effect comparisons; personal loading is not a preparation/constitution fallback. Read admission precedes protected document, source, resource, difference, comparison and prior-act body access. Effect admission is separate from prior read permission.

An authorized-consequence constitution consumes a separately governed prior human act. It checks exact target, issuer/support continuity, current executor, scope, purpose, catalog, treatment, express authorization and controlled source. A proposal, selector or rehashed packet is not that act. This delivery consumes the controlled input; it does not implement an upstream act-authoring interface.

C9 compares the authorized exact candidate/version, content, conditions and context, with recorded human distinction. Equal bytes do not establish identity. Repetition adds provenance as a relationship without overwriting the existing candidate. A declared successor is explicit. Identity collision and possible duplicate remain typed unresolved records with a declared FN-AMBITO destination vacancy, not fabricated approval, responsible person or adjudication.

A stable slot binds preparation revision and logical item across client keys and proposal wrappers. Ordered item/identity locks and database uniqueness prevent a fresh wrapper from allocating another effect. Independent items have a positive overlap control. Intention identity remains deployment + stable principal + act/variant + client key; object/revision belong to the compared canonical payload.

### Persistence and recovery

Migrations 008/009 are additive. Prepared bytes, associations, differences, proposals, effects, outcomes and candidates have append-only permissions/constraints. Data restoration does not restore authority or overwrite live control. The finite populated restore uses the existing independently retained anchor and private-object mechanism; corrupted or missing archive content is not admitted because it carries recalculated metadata.

Actual restart replaces a killed process and recovers the same preparation/effect under current authority without extracting or constituting again. The populated restore includes resources, original/extraction history, current withdrawal and a fresh accepted invitation. This is an exercised controlled recovery path, not an automatic administrative endpoint or universal backup-capacity guarantee.

## Temporal observations and carried limitations
